Services

What I build for businesses

Systems that replace manual process, not tools bolted onto it.

AI Agents

Autonomous systems that handle multi-step tasks across your existing tools — qualifying leads, processing documents, routing requests — without a person driving each step.

Workflow Automation

End-to-end automation of the processes your team repeats daily. Onboarding, invoicing, reporting, CRM updates — connected and running on their own.

Voice AI

Voice agents that answer inbound calls, capture what the caller needs, book appointments, and hand off cleanly to your team when a human is required.

Lead Automation

Pipelines that find, score, and qualify leads on a schedule, then surface only the ones worth a conversation to the people who need to act on them.

Business Process Automation

Document review, compliance checks, data entry, internal approvals. The operational work that scales badly with headcount and well with systems.

Customer Support Automation

Chat and email systems that classify incoming requests, answer what they can, and escalate the rest with full context already attached.
